About Me

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree at New York University (NYU). Currently, I am a Research Intern at Harvard University. I am honored to be supervised by Dr. Kaichen Zhou and Prof. Mengyu Wang in the Harvard AI and Robotics Lab. Previously, I was a Research Intern at the Human Machine Intelligence (HMI) Lab at Peking University under the guidance of Sixiang Chen and Prof. Shanghang Zhang. I received my dual Bachelor's degrees from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) and the University of Glasgow (UofG).

In industry, I have interned at the Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics (X-Humanoid) and Tencent. I am also curious about finance, and have interned at Yuntai Capital in Beijing and Cherami Investment Group in Guangzhou.

My research interests include Agentic AI, Embodied AI, World Models, 3D Vision, and FinTech.
